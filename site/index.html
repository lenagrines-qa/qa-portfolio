TC-01 · ABOUT

I break things on purpose. Then I make sure they stay fixed.

Senior QA Engineer with 9 years of reliable manual and automated testing for web and mobile — telecom, e-commerce, and edtech. I still love a good exploratory pass. I also like a Playwright suite that fails for a real reason, not because somebody renamed a button.

Quality goes through Agile SDLC, and more recently Agentic SDLC with Cursor: story analysis, test planning, agentic testing, Playwright and JavaScript/TypeScript generation, reporting, and skill-based repair of existing automation. Developers write the happy path. I live on the other ones.

Currently

Leading QA on concurrent e-commerce web and mobile at WTS. Partnering with onshore product and development, leading offshore QA, and owning the defect lifecycle in Jira — including Salesforce VIP CRM and AI chatbots that should not hallucinate their way to a live agent.
